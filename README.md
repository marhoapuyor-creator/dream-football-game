# ⚽ Dream Football Game

A browser-based football/soccer game where you can build your own dream team and compete against AI opponents. Features real players like Messi, Ronaldo, Mbappé, Neymar, Haaland, De Bruyne, Vinicius Jr and many more!

## 🎮 Features

- **Build Your Dream Team** - Select 11 players from a database of superstars
- **15+ World-Class Clubs** - Choose from clubs like Manchester City, Real Madrid, Liverpool, PSG, Bayern Munich, Barcelona, and more
- **10-Minute Matches** - Fast-paced gameplay with realistic match mechanics
- **Realistic Players Stats** - Each player has unique attributes:
  - Pace
  - Shooting Accuracy
  - Passing Precision
  - Dribbling Skills
  - Defense Rating
  - Physical Strength
  - Overall Rating

## 🎯 Gameplay Mechanics

### Player Management
1. **Build Your Team** - Select any 11 players from different positions:
   - Strikers (ST)
   - Wingers (LW/RW)
   - Midfielders (CM/LM)
   - Defenders (CB/RB/LB)
   - Goalkeepers (GK)

2. **Match Actions**:
   - 🎯 **SHOOT** - Attempt to score a goal
   - ⚽ **PENALTY** - Take a penalty kick (higher success rate)
   - 🔄 **SWITCH PLAYER** - Change the active player
   - 🎯 **PASS** - Pass the ball to teammates

3. **Real-time Feedback**:
   - Live match events log
   - Score tracking
   - Match timer (10 minutes)
   - Possession percentage
   - Player performance based on their stats

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Open the Game** - Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. **Build Your Team**:
   - Click "Build Your Team"
   - Select your preferred club
   - Choose 11 players from different positions
   - Click "Save Team"
3. **Play a Match**:
   - Click "Play Match"
   - Your team will face a randomly generated opponent
   - Use the action buttons during the match
   - Match lasts 10 minutes
4. **Check Leaderboard** - View your stats and match history

## 📊 Player Stats Explained

- **Pace**: Player's speed on the field
- **Shooting**: Accuracy and power when shooting
- **Passing**: Accuracy of passes
- **Dribbling**: Ball control and agility
- **Defense**: Defensive positioning and tackling
- **Physical**: Strength and stamina
- **Overall**: Combined rating (0-99)
